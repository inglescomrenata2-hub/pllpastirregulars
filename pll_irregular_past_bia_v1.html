Grammar box

Affirmative: Alison hid in the barn.  (hide → hid)

Negative: didn't + base verb → Alison didn't hide in the barn.

Question: Did + subject + base verb? → Did Alison hide in the barn?  Yes, she did. / No, she didn't.

Question word: Where did Alison hide?

After did / didn't, the verb goes back to the base form.  ✗ Did she hid?  ✓ Did she hide?

Exercise 2 — Negatives and questions

For negatives, write didn't + verb. For questions, write did in the first gap and the base verb in the second gap.

  1. Aria (not / say) the truth to her mom.
  2. Hanna (not / buy) a new dress.
  3. Toby (not / come) to the party.
  4. The girls (not / leave) Rosewood.
  5. Emily (not / know) the answer.
  6. Aria (meet) Ezra at the party?
  7. What the girls (hide) in the barn?
  8. Hanna (lose) her phone?
  9. Where Spencer (drive) last night?
  10. Mona (give) the box to Emily?
1. didn't say  |  2. didn't buy  |  3. didn't come  |  4. didn't leave  |  5. didn't know  |  6. Did … meet  |  7. did … hide  |  8. Did … lose  |  9. did … drive  |  10. Did … give
Also correct: did not + verb.
